HOW TO PLAY

Set your target power and horizontal aim, then press START METER. Tap KICK when the moving marker enters the highlighted zone. The zone narrows and the wind gets stronger with each kick. You can also swipe up on the football to set your power and aim, then swipe again to kick when the marker reaches the zone. Perfectly timed field goals earn bonus points.
